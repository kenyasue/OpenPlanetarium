# Privacy Policy — Open Planetarium

**Last updated: 9 August 2026**

Open Planetarium ("the app") is developed by Cats AI d.o.o. / Ken Yasue ("we").
This policy explains what the app does with your information.

**Summary: the app collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sent to
us, and we have no servers that receive your data.**

## Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal
information. The app has no user accounts, no registration, no login, no
crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK, and no analytics SDK.

## Location

If you choose to use the "current location" feature, the app asks your device
for your approximate location. This is used **only on your device**, to compute
which stars, planets, and deep-sky objects are above your horizon.

- Your location is never transmitted to us or to any third party.
- It is not stored anywhere except in the app's own local settings on your
  device, so the app can restore your observing site next time you open it.
- Granting location permission is entirely optional. If you decline, the app
  works exactly the same — you can pick your observing site from the built-in
  world map of 1,250+ cities, or enter coordinates manually.

You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device only. It is never uploaded.

- Display and observing preferences (magnitude limit, light-pollution level,
  visible layers, language for object names)
- Your selected observing location and time settings
- Telescope, camera, eyepiece, and Barlow/reducer entries you create in the
  equipment / field-of-view simulator
- Cached sky-survey image tiles you have downloaded (see below)

Deleting the app removes all of this data.

## Network connections

The full star, constellation, and deep-sky catalog is bundled with the app and
works completely offline. The app only makes network requests when you
explicitly ask it to:

- **Sky-survey imagery (DSS2 layers)** — image tiles are fetched on demand from
  the Centre de Données astronomiques de Strasbourg (CDS) at
  `alasky.cds.unistra.fr` and cached on your device for offline re-display.
- **Optional star-catalog downloads** — additional catalog data may be fetched
  from the VizieR service at `tapvizier.cds.unistra.fr`.

These are anonymous requests for public astronomical data. They contain no
identifying information about you or your device beyond what any web request
necessarily reveals to the receiving server (such as your IP address), and they
are handled under CDS's own privacy practices, not ours. We receive nothing.
All connections use HTTPS.

## Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including
children under 13. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no social features,
and no user-generated content.
